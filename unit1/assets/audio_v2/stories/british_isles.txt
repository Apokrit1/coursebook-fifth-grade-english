Mark writes a presentation about the British Isles. The British Isles are situated on the west side of Europe and consist of two main islands: Great Britain and Ireland. The United Kingdom includes England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland. London is the capital city with over 10 million people. Each country has a proud national flower. The national flower of England is the red rose; for Wales, the bright daffodil; for Scotland, the purple thistle; and for Ireland, the green shamrock. Mark also describes the classic black cabs that drive through London streets.
