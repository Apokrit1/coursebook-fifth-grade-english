Kostas and his classmates learn about friends living in different European countries. Pablo is from Portugal and he is Portuguese. Svetlana is Russian and lives in Moscow. Hans is Dutch and lives in Holland. Carmen is Spanish, Gunther is German, and Maria is Swiss. Armando lives in Albania and Isabella is Italian. Each pupil displays the colourful flag of their nation and shares a morning greeting in their language, from 'Bonjour' in French to 'Buon giorno' in Italian and 'Dobroye utro' in Russian.
