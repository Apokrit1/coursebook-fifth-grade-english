## 1. Distractor Pedagogical Architecture & Principles

All distractors in this dataset are generated strictly following the **Distractor Rules Skill** (`.agents/rules/distractor.md`):

1. **Strict Part-of-Speech (POS) Homogeneity (MANDATORY)**:
   - Noun targets are distracted *exclusively* by nouns.
   - Verb targets are distracted *exclusively* by verbs.
   - Adjective targets (such as nationalities) are distracted *exclusively* by adjectives.
   - Proper noun countries are distracted *exclusively* by proper noun countries.
   - Phrases are distracted *exclusively* by verb/noun phrases.
   *Diagnostic Value*: Prevents learners from bypassing vocabulary retrieval using grammatical category deduction.

2. **Semantic Field Consistency (MANDATORY)**:
   - Hardware words (`mouse`, `screen`) compete against fellow hardware devices.
   - Nationalities (`British`, `French`) compete against other European nationalities.
   - National flowers (`rose`, `daffodil`, `thistle`, `shamrock`) compete against fellow British Isles emblems.
   *Cognitive Load*: Requires precise semantic discrimination rather than superficial recognition.

3. **Curricular & Multi-Unit Pooling**:
   - Fallback items (e.g. for isolated verbs or phrases) are drawn from adjacent categories or the CEFR 5th Grade curriculum lexical database.

4. **Zero-Giveaway Guarantee**:
   - Balanced option lengths, no Greek cognate leaks, and no visual or emoji giveaways in quiz buttons.
