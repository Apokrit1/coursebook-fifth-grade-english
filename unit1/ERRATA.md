# Unit 1 Errata & Pedagogical Reconciliation Notes

What the printed books get wrong or state datedly for Unit 1 (*Internet Friends Around Europe*), and how the digital companion reconciles each item.

Books: **PB** = Pupil's Book (*Βιβλίο Μαθητή*), **AB** = Activity Book / Workbook (*Τετράδιο Εργασιών*), **TB** = Teacher's Book (*Βιβλίο Εκπαιδευτικού*).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | PB p. 18 | "I'm a student in the second year of Collège. That's like your middle school." | French school system terminology: In France, *Collège* is lower secondary school (ages 11–15, matching Greek Gymnasium). In the UK/US, "college" refers to tertiary or university education. | annotate | "Your book says: 'second year of Collège'. In France, Collège is lower secondary school for pupils aged 11–15, equivalent to the Greek Gymnasium." | approved |
| E2 | PB p. 22 | "The population of the United Kingdom is about 59 million." | Demographic figures from 2004 edition. ## Resolution Definitions
- **keep**: Left as printed in the coursebook text.
- **annotate**: Pupil-facing "Book check" badge beside the item, plus full pedagogical note in Teacher Notes drawer.
- **teacher-note**: Explanation placed in Teacher Notes drawer only.
- **approved**: Pedagogical reconciliation accepted and implemented in digital companion data.
