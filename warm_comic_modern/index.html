The Companion

A targeted lexical support module paired with each unit. Provides interactive flashcards, audio pronunciation, 4-option listening challenges, definition quizzes, and printable study sheets — creating a direct bilingual bridge between target English terminology and Greek lexical equivalents.

  • Vocabulary flashcards & lexical drills
  • Greek translations, example sentences & studio audio
  • 4-option multiple choice listening & definition quizzes
  • Structured bilingual lexical reference

Pedagogical Scope & Methodology
Age-Appropriate Design for Grade 5 (Ages 10–11)

The Coursebook delivers an immersive English learning experience with high-interest narrative dialogue, communicative tasks, and authentic cultural realia. The accompanying Vocabulary Companion supports lexical acquisition through systematic bilingual reference.

Coursebook · every lesson

Grammar, reading, listening, culture, CLIL, printable A4 worksheets. In English. Always.

Companion · Lexical Reference

Vocabulary flashcards & lexical-pair drills providing clear Greek-English terminology mapping.

Two-Track Visual Pedagogy

Real open-source realia for real places & habits; inked graphic novel art for classroom scenes.
