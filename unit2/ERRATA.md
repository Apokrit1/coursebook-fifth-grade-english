# Unit 2 Errata & Pedagogical Reconciliation Notes

What the printed books get wrong or state datedly for Unit 2 (*School Life & The World Around Us*), and how the digital companion reconciles each item.

Books: **PB** = Pupil's Book (*Βιβλίο Μαθητή*), **AB** = Activity Book / Workbook (*Τετράδιο Εργασιών*), **TB** = Teacher's Book (*Βιβλίο Εκπαιδευτικού*).

| ID | Book, page | Book says | Problem / Context | Resolution | Pupil Annotation ("Book check") | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | PB p. 26 | Canteen prices: "Muffin 70p, Apple pie 70p, Croissant 50p, Doughnut 50p, Orange juice 60p, Water 30p" | British currency context: Pupils in Greece use Euros (€) and cents. In the UK, 1 pound (£1) = 100 pence (p). | annotate | "Your book says: '70p', '50p'. In the UK, prices are in pence (p), where £1 = 100p (roughly equal to €1.15)." | approved |
| E2 | PB p. 30 | "Interview with a famous basketball player" | Athlete anonymity: The pupil text leaves the basketball star unnamed while TB p. 29 suggests 1980s Greek stars (Galis, Giannakis). | annotate | "Your book keeps the sports star unnamed. You can imagine your favourite modern basketball champion like Giannis Antetokounmpo!" | approved |
| E3 | AB p. 12 / p. 68 | "Henry likes his new school and he's proud of his wand." | Fantasy boarding school theme: Homage to popular fantasy fiction (Harry Potter) with dining hall, potions lab, and magic wands. | keep | Maintained verbatim in the pupil narrative and differentiated reading activity, celebrating pupils' engagement with imaginative storytelling. | approved |
| E4 | PB p. 34 | "What's the difference between habits and customs?" | Linguistic distinction: Habits are individual repeated routines (e.g., brushing teeth, exercising); customs are collective cultural traditions shared by a society (e.g., bowing in Japan, tea in the UK). | annotate | "Habits belong to a person (e.g., I sleep 8 hours). Customs belong to a whole country or culture (e.g., bowing in Japan)." | approved |

## Resolution Definitions
- **keep**: Left as printed in the coursebook text.
- **annotate**: Pupil-facing "Book check" badge beside the item, plus full pedagogical note in Teacher Notes drawer.
- **teacher-note**: Explanation placed in Teacher Notes drawer only.
- **approved**: Pedagogical reconciliation accepted and implemented in digital companion data.
