All distractors in this dataset are generated strictly following the **Distractor Rules Skill** (`.agents/rules/distractor.md`):

1. **Strict Part-of-Speech (POS) Homogeneity (MANDATORY)**: Nouns compete against nouns, adjectives against adjectives, adverbs against adverbs, verbs against verbs.
2. **Semantic Field Consistency (MANDATORY)**: School lexis competes with school lexis; feelings compete with feelings; foods with foods; frequency adverbs with frequency adverbs.
3. **Curricular & Multi-Unit Pooling**: Distractors drawn from official CEFR A1- 5th Grade lexis.
4. **Zero-Giveaway Guarantee**: Balanced option lengths, no Greek cognate leaks, and no visual or emoji giveaways in quiz buttons.
