In Lesson 3, pupils explore cultural traditions and breakfast customs across different countries. In the United Kingdom, a traditional morning breakfast features fried eggs, bacon, hot buttered toast, and black tea with milk. In Japan, families eat a hot nutritious meal of steamed rice, fish, and miso soup using wooden chopsticks. Japanese pupils politely bow to greet their teachers and always take off shoes before stepping into a home. In Greece, children enjoy fresh bread with honey, cereal with cold milk, and orange juice. Understanding these customs teaches everyone good manners and intercultural respect.
