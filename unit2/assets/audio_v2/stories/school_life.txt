School life at West Wimbledon is lively and full of activity every weekday. In the morning, pupils ride the yellow school bus to school. On the bus, children experience different feelings: some pupils are happy and excited to see their friends, while others feel tired after waking up early. At nine o'clock, morning classes begin in the classroom where teachers explain history and geography. At break time, pupils run to the library to borrow exciting books, or gather in the playground for games. Mark loves visiting the computer lab to work on his class project.
