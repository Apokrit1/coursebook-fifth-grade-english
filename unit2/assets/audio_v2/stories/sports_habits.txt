Today a famous professional basketball player visits the school gym for an exclusive interview with pupils. The teacher and class welcome their special guest. When asked how he became so successful, the athlete explains that he maintains strict good habits. He sleeps well for eight hours every night and eats a healthy balanced diet with fresh fruit, vegetables, and milk. He trains for six hours each day with his coach to keep fit and practise his shooting. He advises the pupils that regular exercise, drinking plenty of water, and avoiding junk food are essential for every young person.
