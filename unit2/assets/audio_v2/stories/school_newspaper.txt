Mark attends West Wimbledon Primary School in London. Today, pupils read the new edition of the school newspaper. In the first column, Nadine sends an excited email from Marseilles about her upcoming school trip to Eurodisney by high-speed TGV train. She is sad because her best friend is ill and cannot come. On the right, the school canteen publishes its autumn menu: muffins and apple pie for seventy pence, croissants and doughnuts for fifty pence, and cold bottled water for thirty pence. At the bottom, the headmistress Mrs Clark invites all parents and children to the annual Halloween party on Tuesday October 31st in the school playground.
