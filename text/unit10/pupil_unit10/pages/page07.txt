                  C          DEBATE: RETURNING THE PARTHENON MARBLES
               Should the Parthenon Marbles be returned to Greece or
               not? Now, it is your turn to discuss and decide. You can
               start a debate between two groups of students. The ﬁrst
               group should support the return of the marbles and the
               second group should be against it. Choose a chairperson,
               make some notes and start the debate.
