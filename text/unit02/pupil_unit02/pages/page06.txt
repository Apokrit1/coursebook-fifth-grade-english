                  Lesson 2 TALKING ABOUT HABITS - GOOD & BAD

                  A LEAD-IN 8
              Do you like sports? Do you know any famous sports person, e.g. a runner, a basketball player or a football
              player? Together with your partner think of one or two questions you want to ask him or her.



                  B LISTENING
              Mark listens to a Eurosport radio interview with a famous sports person.
