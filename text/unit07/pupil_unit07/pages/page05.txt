                  D    MORE FAMOUS PEOPLE FROM THE PAST
                  Work in groups. One pupil, the game leader, chooses a famous person. The rest of the group make
                  statements and must find out who the person is. For each correct statement the game leader
                  says yes and for each wrong statement he/she says no.
                  E.g. (for Onassis): “He was a famous shipowner”; “Yes”.
