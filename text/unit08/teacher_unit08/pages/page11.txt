Showing Your Shadows
Set your theater up with a lamp a few
feet behind your screen. Sit on a short
stool or kneel on the floor at point B to
operate your puppets. (NOTE: You might
want to put a tablecloth or drape over the
table front to hide your actions from the
audience.) Hold your puppets up at point A
and the audience (at point D) will see the
shadows on the other side of the screen. As
in most theaters, turning out unnecessary
room lights will help the audience focus on
the on-screen action.
Find or make up a fun story script and
create as many puppets as it takes to tell
the story. You can create shadow scenery
too - including trees, buildings, bridges, and
anything else with a recognizable shadow
shape. You can tape the scenery pieces
in place - to the chair or the back of the
screen, wherever they need to be between
the light and the screen.
