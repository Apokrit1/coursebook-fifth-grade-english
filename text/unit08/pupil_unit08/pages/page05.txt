                 H    WRITE A PLAY AND PERFORM A PUPPET SHOW
               1. Write the small play in 3 Acts. Work as a group and with your partners you can write the rest of the story.
               Prepare the dialogues and try them out using your puppets. When you have it all ready you can add it in your
               portfolio. Later on, when you have prepared a small stage and added some music you can play your puppet
               show in front of the class. Make a puppet, glue a face and clothes on to the body, according to the character
               you want to have. From a paper box make a stage. Your teacher could also videotape the performance and let
               you put it in your Portfolio.
