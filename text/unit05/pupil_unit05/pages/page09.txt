                                                   Mediterranean Forests
                     The Mediterranean is one of the most important regions in the world for its forests. These forests are
                     situated in an area between the European, African and Asian continents and are very important for their
                     biodiversity features – plants and animals.
                     But the Mediterranean forests are under threat. One of the most serious
                     threats is fires. This has caused serious problems in the area during the
                     last few decades. Other general threats to the Mediterranean forests are:
                     • Climate change
                     • Droughts and floodings
                     • Extensive building development
                     • Atmospheric pollution
