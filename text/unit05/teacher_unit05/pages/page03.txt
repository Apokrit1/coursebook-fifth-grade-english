Mark: Wow, that’s great, Kostas! Are you
going to hold an exhibition at school?

persuade our friends and our teachers to
recycle paper, aluminium, glass and plastic.
Mark: Oh, I haven’t thought of recycling

Kostas: Yes, of course it is. Everything needs
to be possible if we want to save our planet.
So, tomorrow morning we’re handing out
leaflets and brochures giving information

take recycling seriously. If we succeed, we’ll
manage to do something about our future.
Mark: That’s a terrific idea! I think I’ll try it

know as soon as I have news. But, please, do
let me know about your results.
Kostas: Ok, I will. I must get back to work
now. Talk to you soon.
Mark: Yes, talk to you tomorrow!
