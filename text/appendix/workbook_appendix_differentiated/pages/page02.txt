               Use Simple Present tense
               1. Where (you/come from) ………………………………………………………………….. ?
                  I come from Greece.
               2. (an elephant/climb)…………………………………………………. trees?
                   No, of course not.
               3. What (a doctor/do)…………………………………………………….?
                   He/She works in a hospital and takes care of people who are ill.
               4. How often (you/visit) …………………………….. a museum?
                  I visit a museum twice a year, at least.
               5. I (like)……………………my teacher because she (tell)……………
                  jokes and(make)………………………..us laugh.
                  I also love my teacher when she doesn’t give us much homework.
               6. Where (a postman/work)…………………………………………………..?
                  He works at the Post Office and delivers letters all over the neighbourhood.
