LESSON 1

DO YOU LIKE COMPUTERS?
Aims of the Lesson
a. To revise and present vocabulary related
to computers and the educational uses of
the Internet.
b. To familiarize pupils with the format of
an e-mail.
c. To get pupils acquainted with the culture
of the Internet.
d. To present and practice structures that
express likes/dislikes/preferences.
